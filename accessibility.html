Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Solvix.S Company (“SOLVIX”) wants everyone, including people who use assistive technology, to be able to use solvixcompany.com comfortably. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What We Have Done

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks;
  • Text alternatives and ARIA labels for interactive elements such as the photo gallery and menu;
  • Keyboard access to navigation, gallery controls, and the quote form;
  • High-contrast text on a dark background and readable font sizes;
  • A layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and screen magnification.

Known Limitations

The project gallery is photo-heavy; we keep descriptions brief and are improving alternative text over time. If any part of the Site is hard for you to use, we want to know.

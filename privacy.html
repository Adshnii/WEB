Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Solvix.S Company (“SOLVIX,” “we,” “us”), a construction and remodeling company based in Naples, Florida, handles information collected through the website solvixcompany.com (the “Site”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us. When you use the quote request form or contact us by email or phone, you provide information such as your name, phone number, email address, and the details of your project. Providing it is voluntary.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited). We do not combine these logs with your identity.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare estimates;
  • To communicate about scheduled or ongoing work;
  • To keep normal business records (invoices, project files).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not send marketing newsletters.

3. Service Providers

The Site relies on a small number of third-party services that process data on our behalf:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (server logs);
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) — delivers quote-form submissions to our email;
  • Google — we may use Google Analytics to understand anonymous, aggregate site usage. Analytics data does not identify you personally.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

The Site itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics is enabled, it may use cookies to count visits anonymously. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing access to any part of the Site.

5. Data Retention

Inquiry emails and project records are kept only as long as needed for business, tax, and legal purposes, after which they are deleted.

6. Sharing

We share personal information only with the service providers listed above, or if required by law (for example, a valid subpoena).

7. Your Choices

You may ask us at any time to show, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email info@solvixcompany.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The Site is intended for adults seeking construction services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this Policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
